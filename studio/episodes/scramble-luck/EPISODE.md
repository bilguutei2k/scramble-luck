# How much of a record-speed Rubik's Cube solve is the shared scramble?

Episode ID: `scramble-luck`

## Question and purpose

Fans often call a record solve "a lucky scramble". In a WCA final every
finalist solves the same five random scrambles, so the other finalists' times
show how much a scramble helped everyone. On average, how much does that
shared scramble/slot component speed up season-best final solves? The viewer
should see why one lucky-looking reconstruction cannot answer this, what the
shared-scramble comparison measures, and what it cannot measure (personal luck
such as a skip in one's own solution, execution, slot conditions).

No verdict is prescribed. The pass/fail rule, written before the confirmation
data was opened, is in `METHOD.md`.

## Scope

Official WCA results (export dated 2026-09-30), 3×3 average-of-five finals with
one scramble group, 2019–2025, in the 80% of competitions held out from the
idea-stage pilot (`data/confirmation-competition-ids.txt`). The pilot's 20%
(`data/exploration-competition-ids.txt`) is exploratory only; its work is in
`../../../ideation/rounds/r3/pilot-S1/` and `../../../ideation/rounds/r5/pilot-S1/`.
